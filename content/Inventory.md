# Inventory

Inventory is the stock held between supply and demand. It improves availability, but it also consumes cash, space, labor, and capacity.

## Key trade-off

More inventory can protect service levels and uncertainty. Less inventory can reduce carrying cost and obsolescence. The right level depends on demand, variability, lead time, risk, and service expectations.
