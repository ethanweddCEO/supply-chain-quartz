# Purchasing

## Definition

Purchasing is the transaction-focused work of obtaining goods and services at the right price, quality, quantity, and time.

## Why it matters

Purchasing turns an approved need into an order that a supplier can fulfill. Strong purchasing controls cost, confirms requirements, tracks delivery, and creates reliable records for receiving and payment.

## What purchasing is not

Purchasing is not the entire procurement function. Procurement also includes strategy, sourcing, supplier evaluation, contracts, risk, and performance management.
