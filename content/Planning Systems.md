# Planning Systems

Planning systems convert demand and supply information into actionable timing and quantity decisions.

## Core terms

- [[Glossary#ERP|ERP]]
- [[Glossary#MRP|MRP]]
- [[Glossary#Net requirements|Net requirements]]
- [[Glossary#Projected available balance|Projected available balance]]
- [[Glossary#Lead time|Lead time]]

## Planning sequence

Demand is translated into gross requirements. Existing inventory and scheduled receipts are considered. Shortfalls become net requirements, which trigger planned receipts and releases after lead-time offsets.
