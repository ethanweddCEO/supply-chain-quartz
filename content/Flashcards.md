# Flashcards

Create a personal study deck without an account. Your cards and quiz history are stored only in this browser using local storage.

The built-in deck includes core terms from the Supply Chain Dictionary. Add your own terms below to practice class notes, interview preparation, or certification material.

On glossary and topic pages, use **Add to quiz** beside a definition to place that term at the front of your next quiz. The selected term is marked **Next quiz** in your local deck and is removed from the queue after it is practiced.

> [!tip] Privacy by design
> This tool does not create an account or transmit your cards. Clearing this browser's site data removes your personal deck and history.
