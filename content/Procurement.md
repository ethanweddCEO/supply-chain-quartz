# Procurement

Procurement manages the full commercial and operational process of acquiring goods and services.

## Scope

1. Define the business need.
2. Analyze spend and requirements.
3. Source and evaluate suppliers.
4. Negotiate terms and manage contracts.
5. Issue and manage purchase orders.
6. Measure supplier performance and total cost.
