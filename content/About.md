# About the Supply Chain Dictionary

The Supply Chain Dictionary is a practical learning reference built from Obsidian notes, class study, and ongoing review.

## What you will find

- **Definitions** for common supply chain terms
- **Class notes** with examples, questions, and working explanations
- **Resources** for courses, systems, and further study
- **Flashcards** for private, browser-only practice

## How it is maintained

Obsidian remains the source of truth. New notes, tags, aliases, and links can be added there and published through Quartz without a database or account system.

The dictionary is intentionally a work in progress. Definitions should become clearer over time as examples, calculations, and related concepts are added.

## A note about study content

These pages are learning notes, not professional advice. Verify operational, financial, legal, and compliance decisions against your organization's policies and qualified sources.
