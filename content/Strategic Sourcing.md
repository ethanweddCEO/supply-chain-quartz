# Strategic Sourcing

Strategic sourcing evaluates suppliers and commercial choices beyond the quoted unit price.

## Decision factors

- Total cost of ownership
- Quality and service
- Capacity and lead time
- Supply continuity and risk
- Contract terms
- Supplier capability and relationship fit
