# Supplier Management

Supplier management aligns internal stakeholders and suppliers around performance, communication, risk, quality, cost, and long-term value.

## What supplier management is not

It is not simply a supplier portal or a purchasing transaction. Effective supplier management is cross-functional and continues after the purchase order is issued.
